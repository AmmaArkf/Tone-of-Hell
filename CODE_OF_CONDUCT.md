# Code of Conduct

We want everyone to feel comfortable in this project: musicians, programmers and people just starting out. This code applies everywhere the project is present in public: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel that represents the project.

## What we expect

- Respect others. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Explain your thoughts clearly and patiently, especially to newcomers.
- Accept feedback calmly and thank people for their help.
- Remember that people have different experience, languages and time zones. Not everyone writes English equally well.
- Submit only what you have the right to submit.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, humiliation, bullying and harassment.
- Discrimination on any basis.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.
- Spam and advertising unrelated to the project.
- Deliberately violating other people's copyrights or licenses.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## What happens when the code is broken

Maintainers may warn a participant, remove a comment or an issue, and restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Decisions are made case by case, with an explanation when possible.

## How to report a violation

Send a private message to the maintainer on GitHub (profile `@AmmaArkf`). Please do not discuss violations publicly. We review every report and keep the reporter's identity confidential as far as possible.
